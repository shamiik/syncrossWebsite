Frequently Asked Questions

Industrial IoT (IIoT) refers to the network of interconnected devices, machines, sensors, and software used in various industries to collect, exchange, and analyze data. It enables businesses to optimize processes, enhance productivity, and make data-driven decisions.

Syncross is an Industrial IoT and AI platform that connects machines, utilities, and production data into one operating layer. Our solutions cover energy management, utility management, production enhancement, OEE monitoring, maintenance management, and compressor management.

IIoT gives you real-time visibility into your plant, so you can find energy and utility waste, reduce unplanned downtime, improve equipment effectiveness, and make faster decisions based on live data instead of manual reports.

Security is part of how we design and deploy our platform, including controlled access to data and dashboards. Contact our team for details about the security measures that apply to your deployment and your plant's requirements.
