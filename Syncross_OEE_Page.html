Production Challenges

Production Lines Are Running. But Are They Performing at Their Best?

Hidden downtime, slow cycles, minor stoppages, late defect detection, and manual reporting reduce output before management can react.

Hidden Downtime

Machine stoppages remain unclear without real-time downtime tracking and reason classification.

Speed Loss

Slow cycles and minor stops reduce throughput even when machines appear to be running.

Manual Reporting

Manual production reports delay action and create gaps in target vs actual visibility.

Late Quality Signals

Rejects and rework become expensive when quality loss is not connected to production performance.

No Machine Benchmark

Teams cannot compare OEE, output, downtime, and losses by machine, line, or shift.

Weak Target Tracking

Production goals are difficult to control without live target achievement and line efficiency KPIs.

Unclear Root Cause

Recurring production loss continues when downtime reasons and bottlenecks are not structured.

Scattered Data

Machine, PLC, SCADA, HMI, operator, and quality data stay disconnected across the floor.

Simple Factory Language

Understand OEE: Availability × Performance × Quality

OEE becomes powerful when teams can clearly see where losses are happening: time loss, speed loss, or quality loss.

Availability

How much of scheduled production time the machine was actually running. Track downtime, breakdowns, waiting time, changeover loss, and stoppage reasons.

×

Performance

How fast the machine or line is running compared to ideal speed. Identify slow cycles, minor stops, and throughput gaps.

×

Quality

How many good products were produced compared to total production. Track rejects, rework, defect trends, and quality-related production loss.

OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality
