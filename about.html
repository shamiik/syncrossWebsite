What is Syncross

A complete ecosystem, built by the Sincos R&D team

Syncross is a complete Industrial IoT & AI ecosystem designed and developed by the Sincos R&D team to help industries collect real-time data from machines, sensors, PLCs, meters, utilities, and production systems.

It connects industrial assets into one unified digital platform where management, engineering, maintenance, production, and utility teams can monitor performance, analyze data, identify losses, and take faster decisions.

MachinesSensorsPLCsMetersUtilitiesProduction systems

Syncross helps industries to

  • Monitor machines, utilities, energy, production, compressors, and maintenance in real time

  • AI-powered analytics for trend, anomaly, performance gaps and predictive insights

  • Reduce operational cost, energy loss, downtime, and wastage

  • Improve productivity, quality, compliance, and resource utilization

  • Generate automatic reports in Excel and PDF

  • Access dashboards from web, mobile, and management systems

  • Make intelligent decisions based on real-time and AI-analyzed data

  • Securely access your systems and data from anywhere, anytime on any device

  • Securely scale industrial data in the cloud
